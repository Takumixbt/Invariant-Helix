Rounding direction on withdrawal favours the caller

Severity: High. The vault computes shares to burn on withdrawal using a division that rounds down, while the assets returned round up. An attacker repeatedly withdraws the smallest non-zero amount, each time burning one fewer share than the accounting requires. Over many iterations the share price is dragged down and the residual depositors absorb the loss. Root cause: the rounding direction is not inverted between the deposit and withdraw paths, so the protocol rounds in the user's favour on both sides.

Recommendation: round shares up on withdrawal, mirroring the deposit path.
